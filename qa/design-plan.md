# Design direction: a concert programme becomes an instrument

Palette: porcelain #f2f5f7, midnight blue #102d43, recital blue #1752aa, water blue #cee0ec, copper #ad4b32, silver #7c929f.
Typography: Libre Bodoni for the living title and composer identity; Instrument Sans for controls and programme text, both self-hosted under SIL OFL.
Layout: a spacious left-aligned concert programme opens into a large blue performance stage. The melody's actual notes become a scrolling score landscape. A nine-name composer rail is navigation, not nine identical cards. Programme notes remain typographic and quiet.
Signature: a custom score-field showing authored pitches, voices and phrase shapes, linked to the exact audio time. No decorative stock gradients or unrelated waveform.
Interaction: original first, no autoplay, two clear instrument choices, a single playback transport, meaningful cues, optional relative-progress A/B pin, download MIDI and view source notes.
Review against generic defaults: rejected warm beige/serif/terracotta editorial and black/neon dashboard. The music-specific score field is the one bold element. Explicit names, original-arrangement labels and real phrase notes take priority over marketing adjectives. Copper reserved for the current playhead and emphasis, not callout decoration.
